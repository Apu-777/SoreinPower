## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clean, high-precision aesthetic engineered for next-generation renewable energy commerce—spanning residential solar arrays, battery storage ecosystems, and microgrid hardware. The design merges **minimalism** with a subtle **high-tech technical precision**, communicating architectural reliability, environmental efficacy, and modern engineering standards.

The interface should feel airy, luminous, and meticulously calibrated. It conveys zero visual clutter; instead, it relies on crystalline surface contrast, ample negative space, and laser-sharp typographic hierarchy. The user should experience calm confidence, technological sophistication, and clear actionable paths when exploring complex technical specifications and making high-value sustainable investments.

## Colors

The palette leverages a crisp, sunlit atmospheric foundation paired with electric, clean-tech energetic accents.

- **Primary (`#0D9488` - Deep Teal):** The core active accent used for primary calls-to-action, key telemetry indicators, interactive states, and core branding elements.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Electric Sky/Cyan):** Supporting technical accent used for energy flow visualization, secondary highlights, interactive focus rings, and link interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Emerald Efficiency):** Denotes ecological yield, high-efficiency ratings, operational status metrics, and positive verification tokens.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** High-density charcoal for primary typographic hierarchy, key hardware silhouettes, and structural anchor elements.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#334155` - Slate 700):** Supporting text, secondary labels, and descriptive prose.
- **Background & Surfaces:** Built on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) as the baseline canvas, supported by subtle cool surface tiers (`#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9`) to generate spatial depth without requiring heavy visual ornamentation.

## Typography

The type system blends the technological authority of **Space Grotesk** for display titles, technical specs, numbers, and badge labels, with the human readability and neutral balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for running text, documentation, and interface controls.

- **Headlines & Technical Metrics:** Set with tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver an engineered, monolithic quality reminiscent of industrial control panels and modern architectural interfaces.
- **Body & Continuous Copy:** Kept open and comfortable, utilizing generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to ensure effortless scanning of system specs and warranty conditions.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small-caps technical metrics use widened letter spacing to optimize data legibility across dense spec sheets and configuration tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored around a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with strict geometric alignment:

- **Desktop (1280px and above):** Max container width of 1440px. 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins of `3rem` to create deliberate boundary breathing space.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. Complex e-commerce matrices (such as solar package configurators) shift from horizontal multi-column splits into 2-column or stacked card blocks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Grids collapse to single-column flows; technical comparison matrices unlock horizontal finger-swipe capability.

Spacing rhythm relies exclusively on an 8pt-based progression. High whitespace ratios are preserved across sections to highlight high-fidelity hardware photography and clean energy data visualization.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons artificial skeuomorphic borders and harsh dropshadows in favor of **ambient diffused shadows** and **flat surface contrasts**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Tiles):** Borderless `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC` backgrounds, elevated only by a whisper of diffused shadow tinted with slate undertones: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Dropdowns):** Subtle elevation bloom: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Checkout Drawers, Configurator Overlays):** Dynamic floating lift: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Nesting:** In place of divider borders, secondary groups within cards (such as inverter efficiency stats or add-on modules) sit directly on `#F1F5F9` nested islands with zero outer stroke.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature. This introduces smooth `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii on standard controls (buttons, inputs, chips) scaling up to `1rem` (`16px`) on product cards, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) on major hero modules and banners.

The geometry avoids exaggerated pill shapes for structural cards, maintaining a precise, architectural silhouette that reflects the physical industrial design of clean energy battery packs, solar arrays, and smart inverters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0D9488` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text (`label-md`), `0.5rem` radius, zero border. On hover, background shifts toward `#0F766E` with a slight ambient glow.
- **Secondary:** Surface tint `#F1F5F9` with `#0F172A` text. On hover, fills with `#E2E8F0`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#0D9488` or `#334155` text with `0.5rem` hit-area; text-underline or background wash appears strictly on hover.

### Product & Spec Cards
- **Construction:** Pure borderless design. Body sits on `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 ambient elevation against `#F8FAFC`.
- **Structure:** Generous padding (`space-lg`), high-clarity hardware renders placed against transparent neutral backdrops, followed by a crisp Space Grotesk title, dynamic capacity metrics (e.g., `13.5 kWh`), and nested `#F1F5F9` spec capsules.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (e.g., "Active Grid", "In Stock"):** Pill-rounded (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, utilizing light semantic tints: `#059669` text on `#ECFDF5` background.
- **Filter Chips:** Light gray outline-free `#F1F5F9` surfaces. Selected state transitions to solid `#0F172A` text on `#0D9488` with 15% opacity background, or crisp solid `#0D9488` with white text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Background of `#F8FAFC`, no heavy default border. A 1px subtle tint `#E2E8F0` keeps form bounds intact without visual noise. Focus states trigger a crisp 2px outer ring in `#0284C7` (Electric Sky) with `#FFFFFF` field background.
- **Labels:** Positioned cleanly above using `label-md` in `#334155`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp `0.25rem` (checkbox) and circular (radio) controls with an unticked `#E2E8F0` border. Active state features a solid `#0D9488` fill with a sharp white checkmark or center pip.

### Clean Energy Additions
- **Telemetry Metric Blocks:** Modular telemetry cards displaying real-time power generation (kW), battery percentage (%), and grid self-reliance ratios. Numeric values are rendered in Space Grotesk Display sizes with subtle colored indicator dots (emerald, teal, or sky).
- **Energy Flow Sliders & Calculators:** Interactive capacity adjusters with track fills transitioning smoothly from `#0D9488` to `#0284C7`, equipped with precise micro-metric tooltips.
